### SELMA3D 2026: Self-supervised learning for 3D light-sheet microscopy image segmentation

SELMA3D 2026 is the third edition of the SELMA3D challenge, held as an in-person satellite event at [MICCAI 2026](https://conferences.miccai.org/2026/en/) in Strasbourg, France. It benchmarks self-supervised learning for segmenting biological structures in 3D light-sheet microscopy (LSM) images. The 2026 edition adds macaque and human axon LSM data from the NIH [BRAIN CONNECTS program](https://www.brain-connects.org/).
